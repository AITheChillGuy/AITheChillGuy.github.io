§ 01 — About

Computer engineer building practical AI systems.

I'm a computer engineer doing AI research. The work splits across a few things — data preprocessing pipelines, pretraining and fine-tuning language models, RAG systems, multimodal models, and AI agents that use tools. Usually the whole thing end-to-end, because the interesting failures tend to live in the seams between components.

My current role is at Weill Cornell Medicine — Qatar, a medical college in Doha. A lot of my data is clinical because of that, not because the work itself is about medicine. A fine-tuning loop or RAG pipeline doesn't change much when you point it at contracts or patents instead.

I came up through Qatar University as a computer engineer. My path into AI wasn't linear: a senior design project on VR and haptic gloves, a research stint on brain MRIs, and then mostly LLMs. None of it was planned at the time, but each detour turned out useful for the next.
