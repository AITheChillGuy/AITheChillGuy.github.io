Multimodal Models for Radiology

Vision-language models that read the scan, not just the chart — visual question answering and automated report generation over 2D and 3D imaging.

Role
AI Research Specialist · WCM-Q
Stack
PyTorch · Hugging Face · DICOM · VQA · LMM
Year
2024 — Present
Status
Ongoing research

The problem. Radiology questions are visual. A text-only model can paraphrase a report but can't read the scan. Real assistance means working with the pixels, not summarizing prior reads.

The approach. Large multimodal models built and evaluated for visual question answering and automated report generation over 2D and 3D medical imaging.

VQA and report generation that work directly over the image — not just the surrounding text.

What was hard. Working across modalities at the same time — getting the vision encoder, the language model, and the medical-image preprocessing to behave as one system rather than three bolted together.

What shipped. A VQA and report-generation pipeline evaluated against clinical tasks.

What this changes. The bar shifts when the model can actually look at the image. The output is no longer a paraphrase of a prior report — it's an answer keyed to what the scan actually shows.

Ongoing research at Weill Cornell Medicine — Qatar. Internal evaluation.
