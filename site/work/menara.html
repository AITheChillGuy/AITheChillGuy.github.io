منارة منارة منارة KUFI — one word, three hands MENARA

نموذج واحد، عربيّات كثيرة·One model, many Arabics

Patients don't speak textbook Arabic. MENARA merges an Egyptian Arabic specialist, a Moroccan Darija specialist, and a medical specialist into one 2-billion-parameter model — no retraining, and it outperforms Arabic models six times its size on dialectal medicine.

The problem. Arabic is not one language at the patient level. Clinical conversations happen in Egyptian, Darija, Gulf, Levantine — not in the Modern Standard Arabic most models are trained on. A specialist per dialect works, but training, storing, and serving a separate medical model for every dialect doesn't scale.

The approach. Don't train — merge. MENARA combines three Gemma-2B specialists (Meditron3 for medicine, an Egyptian Arabic model, Atlas-Chat for Darija) with TIES merging at density 0.6, weighting the dialect specialists 0.6 against the medical backbone's 0.4. Zero-shot: no gradient ever flows.

A 2B merge averages 3.68 on dialectal fidelity where Jais-13B manages 2.84 — dialect adaptation beats scale.

What was hard. Proving the dialect is real, not decorative. Beyond LLM-judged fidelity (Qwen 3, 300 prompts per dialect) and native-speaker ratings, the paper introduces a token-level composition analysis: prompted in Darija, MENARA's replies are 54% Darija with 27% MSA and French borrowings — which is how Darija actually talks about medicine, not leakage. Two medical SMEs reviewed clinical safety; evaluator stability reached Krippendorff's α = 0.89.

What shipped. One 2B model with the most balanced profile across all three varieties (4.89 / 3.02 / 3.12 fidelity), no catastrophic failure in any dialect, cross-dialect answering in all six directions, robustness to noisy input, and more than 60% less storage than maintaining three specialists.

What this changes. Model merging becomes a practical answer to linguistic fragmentation in healthcare: when the next dialect is needed, merge a specialist in — no retraining, no second server, no model zoo.
