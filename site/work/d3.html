01 Pretrain the decoder ＋

Learn from curated DrugBank interaction data using a language-model objective. The released configuration uses six transformer blocks, eight attention heads, and 512-dimensional embeddings.

02 Adapt to the task ＋

Replace the language-model output layer with a three-class head. The released notebook trains the final transformer block, final normalization, and classification head for Minor, Moderate, and Major labels.

03 Compare against larger models ＋

Fine-tune Qwen 2.5, Gemma, Mistral v0.3, and LLaMA 3.1 for the same DDI task. Assess predictions with task metrics and expert evaluation.
